📊 The Pulse of the Region

Charlotte's Economy Is Outrunning the Nation

2.9%

Nonfarm employment growth across metro Charlotte over the past year — nearly 40,000 net new jobs, outpacing Raleigh, Nashville, Austin, and Atlanta.

  

92K/mo

Average national job gains per month in the first half of 2026 — a sharp acceleration from just 10,000/month in 2025.
 

3.6%

Mecklenburg County's unemployment rate — a tight labor market that means the war for talent in LKN isn't cooling off anytime soon.

  

2x

Growth in announced jobs and capital investment across the Charlotte region in Q1 2026 versus Q1 2025.

Construction, professional services, and healthcare are carrying the region's momentum, while leisure and hospitality cooled slightly this summer. Housing is finally giving buyers — and the professionals relocating for these new jobs — a little breathing room, with steadier, more sustainable price growth instead of the frenzy of years past.

🚀 Why it matters for LKN: Growth this fast doesn't wait for your workforce to catch up on its own. Every business, nonprofit, and school in this region has a window right now — to attract talent, retain it, and build the culture that keeps people from leaving the second a bigger offer shows up. That's exactly the work LINK does.

🎒 Back-to-School Check-In

New Backpack. New Shoes. New... Skills?

School's back in session in just a few weeks 🎒 — and soon everyone will be posting first-day-of-school photos. But here's the question nobody's asking at drop-off: is your kid actually learning the skills that get them hired, accepted, or ahead? Grades matter. But so does knowing how to walk into a room, work a team, and land an opportunity before their friends even know it exists.

😬 Real talk: the kids building these skills before senior year even starts are the ones who won't be scrambling come application and internship season. FOMO is real — don't let your student be the one asking "wait, how'd they already get that internship?"

🚀 Launching in Weeks

Future Ready Experience Arrives at Ambassador Christian

Discover Purpose. Build Skills. Launch Your Future.

If you didn't know — now you know: our dedication to youth and to this region's workforce has never been stronger. It's not a pitch anymore. It's in the classroom.

In just a few weeks, LINK brings the workforce directly into the classroom. The Future Ready Experience launches at Ambassador Christian for 5th-year senior athletes — a four-day-per-week career readiness class built around real soft skills training and hands-on internship placement running January through May. This isn't a career-day assembly. It's a semester-long bridge between the classroom and the workplace, designed so students leave with more than a diploma — they leave with direction, experience, and a foot already in the door.
